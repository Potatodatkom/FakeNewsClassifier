CAN YOU TRUST WHAT YOU READ?

TruthScan uses machine learning to examine the language and patterns inside a news article and classify the submitted story as REAL or FAKE.

Instead of checking a single keyword, the system processes the submitted text through a TF-IDF feature extraction pipeline before sending it to a trained Logistic Regression classifier.
